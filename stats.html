What the two numbers mean

Visits is how many times a page was opened. Used it is how many of those did something once they arrived, rather than landing and leaving. The gap between them is the interesting bit.

Family and your own testing count too. Take your own visits off before you believe a number.

No cookies, nothing stored on anyone's device, nothing that identifies a person. Just a number going up.
