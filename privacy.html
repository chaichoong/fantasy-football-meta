Privacy

The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and there is nothing on our side that could identify you.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your built squad and your saved plans are stored in your own browser, on your own device. They never reach us. Clearing your browser data deletes them, and we cannot get them back for you because we never had them.

The "Devices" feature moves that saved setup between your own devices using a code you copy across yourself. Nothing is stored on a server in between.

What we count

We count two things: that a visit happened, and that somebody interacted with the page. That is a number going up by one. No cookie is set, nothing is written to your device for this, and no part of it is linked to you or to any other visit.

Because of that we cannot tell whether you have been here before, and we are content with that trade.

Your fantasy team ID

If you type your fantasy team ID into the "My Team" screen, that number is sent to our relay so it can fetch your public squad from the official Fantasy Premier League feed. It is used for that request and not stored by us. The ID only ever reaches publicly readable pages of the official game, never anything that needs a login.

Who else is involved

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, who keep standard security and traffic logs of requests to any website they serve, as every host does. Data comes from the Premier League's own public Fantasy Premier League feed.

Children

This is a fantasy football tool. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age, so there is nothing held about a child any more than about an adult.

Getting in touch

Questions about this page go to kevinbrittain@gmail.com. Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to send you a copy of or to delete, but ask if you want that confirmed.

Fantasy Football Meta is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier League or the official Fantasy Premier League game.
